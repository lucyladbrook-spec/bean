Did You
Know?

01

Every single borlotti bean has its own completely unique pattern of speckles. Yours is one of a kind.

02

Before the pods appear, borlotti beans grow the most beautiful pink and white flowers.

03

A healthy borlotti plant can grow taller than a grown-up — over two whole metres.
